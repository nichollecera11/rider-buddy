# 🏍️ Rider Buddy: Trust-First Motorcycle Ecosystem

[![Laravel](https://img.shields.io/badge/Framework-Laravel%2011-red)](https://laravel.com)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

**Rider Buddy** is a specialized digital ecosystem designed for the Filipino riding community. Moving beyond simple roadside assistance, it serves as a **Digital Guardian** for motorcycle health, legality, and transparency.

---

## 🛡️ Our Vision: "Kaluwasan ug Transparency sa Dalan"
Our mission is to protect riders from mechanical fraud and expensive LTO fines through verified digital records and expert diagnostics. We believe that every motorcycle should have a transparent "Health History" to ensure safety and maintain its resale value.

---

## ✨ Key Features (The Trust-First Pivot)

### 👨‍🔧 1. Diagnostic-First Consultations
Instead of blind bookings, Rider Buddy implements a professional diagnostic flow.
* **Digital Diagnosis Report:** Mechanics provide a verified digital finding before major repairs.
* **Transparent Fees:** Fixed `diagnostic_fee_base` so riders know what they pay for expertise.

### 📋 2. Verified Health History (Digital Medical Record)
* **Maintenance Logs:** Track oil changes, tire replacements, and engine work.
* **Mechanic Verification:** Records are "signed" by verified mechanics to boost the bike's resale value.
* **Health Meter:** Real-time tracking of parts lifespan (Engine, Tires, Brakes).

### 🚨 3. LTO Compliance Butler
* **Multa Protection:** Automatic reminders for Registration and Insurance expiry to avoid the ₱10,000 LTO fine.
* **Plate & Policy Tracking:** Centralized storage for vehicle legal documents.

### 🏪 4. Integrated Marketplace
* **Verified Listings:** Buy and sell motorcycles with a viewable "Health History" badge.
* **Parts Shop:** Local sellers can highlight their inventory with a "Verified Seller" status.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
* **Backend:** Laravel 11 (PHP 8.2+)
* **Database:** MySQL / PostgreSQL
* **API:** RESTful API with Laravel Sanctum for Mobile Integration
* **Architecture:** Trust-First Transactional Model (Consultations vs. Bookings)
